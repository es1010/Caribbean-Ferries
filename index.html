Ferries do more than move people: in the Lesser Antilles they trace invisible borders. Mapped as networks rather than single routes, they reveal five regional groups shaped by language, history and mobility policies, and show who is connected to whom.

Hover a route or a port for details. Click the legend to filter line types or isolate a network. Click a magnifier on the map to zoom in, and again to zoom out.

Map by Estelle Sérèmes for the #30DayMapChallenge 2026, Day 24: Network. Data: Natural Earth, ferryroutes.com (2025). Made with QGIS; interactions added with CartABl (IGARUN, Nantes Université).
